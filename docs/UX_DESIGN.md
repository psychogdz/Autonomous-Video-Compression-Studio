  - `Prism Mint` (`#00F5A0`): Success state, data telemetry readout, and space savings celebration.

### 2. Button & Control Philosophy
- **Initialize Compression (Primary Trigger):**
  - High-impact luminous aperture button (`btn-aperture-trigger`) with dual-stop cyan/electric gradient, tactile depth, subtle white micro-sheen top border, and active click response.
- **Segmented Console Bar (Mode Selector):**
  - Integrated cockpit console track (`bg-void-900/95`) with smooth glass pill indicators and cyan telemetry dot.
- **Preset Goal Cards (Simple Mode):**
  - Restrained, purposeful cards with distinct harmonic color accents, crisp badges, and zero childish emoji clutter.
- **Target Size Dial & Slider:**
  - Cyan-illuminated range track with glowing optical thumb and live bitrate budget counter.
- **Dual-Frame Split Video Comparison:**
  - High-contrast synchronized scrubbing player with glowing divider handle.
